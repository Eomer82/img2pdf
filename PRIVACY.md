# Privacy Policy for Img2Pdf

Img2Pdf is a local desktop utility designed to convert image files (JPG, PNG, BMP) into high-quality PDF documents.

## Data Collection and Network Transmission

**This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.**

- **No Telemetry:** Img2Pdf does not collect, store, or transmit any analytics, telemetry, or user usage data.
- **Local Processing:** All image-to-PDF conversions are performed 100% locally on your computer.
- **Offline Operation:** The application does not connect to the internet during operation.
- **No Third-Party Services:** No advertising trackers, third-party network analytics, or cloud storage endpoints are integrated.

## Contact

For questions or issues regarding Img2Pdf, please visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Eomer82/img2pdf).
